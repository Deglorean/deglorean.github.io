About Risk Score Calculator

The Risk Score Calculator uses a Fine-Kinney style equation: probability × exposure × consequence. Adjust the inputs to see how they combine into a risk score.

Compare the original risk with the expected risk after controls, then explore how risk reduction and correction cost influence the justification result.

The cost analysis is an adjustable decision-support layer. Its upper limit and value scale should be set to suit the workplace, budget and control priorities; it is not a fixed legal, financial or engineering standard.
