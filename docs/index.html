Why Might Help?

I made something useful. It might help you too.

Most of the things here started because I needed to solve a problem.

Sometimes that meant building a program. Sometimes it was a calculator, a teaching resource, a simulator or simply a better way of explaining something.

Once something works, it seems wasteful for it to help only one person.

So I put it here. Not because every resource will suit everyone, and not because every problem has one perfect answer.

Just because it might help.

UsefulEverything should solve, explain or simplify something.
PracticalResources should be made to use, not just made to look impressive.
AdaptableWhere possible, tools should adjust to the person, task or situation.
AccessibleKeep unnecessary barriers between people and useful resources to a minimum.
